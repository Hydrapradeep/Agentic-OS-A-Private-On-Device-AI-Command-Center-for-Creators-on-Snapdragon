# Agentic OS: A Private, On-Device AI Command Center for Creators on Snapdragon

> Built for the Snapdragon® AI Lab Build & Present Challenge.

Agentic OS is a voice-controlled personal command center. It ranks fresh news for
your niche, drafts and schedules social posts, and runs a JARVIS-style assistant
over your tasks, mail and expenses. Switch to **On-device AI mode** and the AI runs
locally on a Snapdragon-powered PC, so your data stays on your machine.

**Status:** the on-device routing layer is implemented and tested against a mock
local model server. Snapdragon NPU benchmarking is the next step.
